SpiceStack privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what the SpiceStack browser extension and the SpiceStack dashboard at spicestack.labuyo.app collect, where it is kept, and how to delete it. SpiceStack is made by Noel Manalungsung, trading as Labuyo ("Labuyo", "we"). SpiceStack is not affiliated with Apollo.io.

What SpiceStack collects

  • The leads and company details Apollo's search shows you. When you press Export on an Apollo people search, SpiceStack reads the results that Apollo shows you on that page: for each person, name, job title, email address and email status as Apollo shows them, location, and LinkedIn, Twitter and Facebook links; and the person's company details as Apollo shows them. It also keeps the search address and filter name, so the export can be named and its search reopened.
  • These are saved in your browser. The extension stores exports in your browser's extension storage on your computer.
  • If you sign in, they are uploaded to your SpiceStack account, so you can download them from the dashboard at any time and from any browser.
  • Your email address, for signing in. If you create an account, we keep your email address and a securely hashed password. We also keep each export's status, lead counts and start and end times, and your plan and daily usage, to run the free plan's limit.
  • Your plan, if you pay. Payments are handled by Paddle (see Sharing). We receive your plan, its status and renewal date, and Paddle's customer and subscription references. We never see or store your card details.
  • Your acceptance of the risk notice, stored in your browser only.

SpiceStack does not collect your browsing history, and does not read anything from other sites. The extension only runs on Apollo (app.apollo.io) and on the SpiceStack dashboard. SpiceStack does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. When you open checkout, Paddle's checkout script loads on the dashboard and may use cookies it needs for payment and fraud prevention, under Paddle's own privacy policy.

Why

We use this data only to provide SpiceStack's single purpose: exporting the leads your Apollo search shows you, and letting you download them. We do not use it for anything else.

Who is responsible for the leads you export

The people in your exports are chosen by you, from your own Apollo account, for your own purposes. For that lead data:

  • You are the controller. You decide which people to export and what you do with them, so you are responsible for having a lawful basis (for example, legitimate interest for B2B outreach, documented in an assessment), for honouring those people's rights, and for following anti-spam rules when you contact them.
  • Labuyo is your processor. If you sign in, SpiceStack stores the leads only so you can view and download them, on your instructions. We do not use them for ourselves, combine them with other customers' data, or sell them.

For your own account data (email, plan, usage counts), Labuyo is the controller.

Where it is stored

  • In your browser: the extension's local storage, on your computer.
  • For signed-in users: in a database run by Supabase, Inc. on our behalf, in its Tokyo, Japan region. Data is sent over HTTPS. The European Commission recognises Japan as giving personal data an adequate level of protection.
  • The dashboard and labuyo.app are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and the labuyo.app domain and its email forwarding run on Cloudflare, Inc. Both may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.

How long we keep it

  • Exports and their leads are kept until you delete the export or your account.
  • Each account can store up to 100,000 leads. When the limit is reached, new leads are not saved until you delete old exports.
  • Exports in your browser stay until you remove them or uninstall the extension.
  • Paddle keeps payment and invoice records for as long as tax law requires.

Deleting your data

  • One export: press Delete next to it on the dashboard.
  • Everything: on the dashboard, open Account and plan, then Delete my account. This removes your account and every export and lead stored with it. It cannot be undone.
  • In your browser: uninstalling the extension removes the data it stored there.

If you cannot sign in, email support@labuyo.app from the address on the account and we will delete it.

Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone, except the service providers that run SpiceStack for us: Supabase (database), GitHub (website hosting), Cloudflare (domain and email forwarding) and Paddle.com (payments). Paddle is the Merchant of Record for every purchase and handles your payment details as a controller under its own privacy policy. The others may use data only to provide their service to us. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs and collected by this extension will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, SpiceStack:

  • uses the data only to provide its single purpose, described above;
  • does not transfer it to others except as needed to provide that purpose, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, with notice to you;
  • does not use or transfer it for advertising, including personalised advertising;
  • does not use or transfer it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending; and
  • does not allow humans to read it, except with your consent, for security reasons, to comply with the law, or when it is aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Email support@labuyo.app to use these rights.

Changes

If we change what SpiceStack collects, we will update this page and tell you in the extension before the change takes effect.
