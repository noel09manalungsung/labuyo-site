SpiceStack

Browser extension for Chrome and Edge

SpiceStack exports the leads your own Apollo people search shows you, to CSV or Excel. Large searches are split into batches automatically, and signed-in users can download every export again from the dashboard.

  • Export from the Apollo tab you are on, or run large exports in batches
  • Files named by date, Apollo filter name and batch
  • Stop an export at any time and download what was collected
  • Delete an export, or your whole account, from the dashboard
