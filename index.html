SpiceStack

Browser extension for Chrome and Edge

SpiceStack exports the leads your own Apollo people search shows you, to CSV or Excel. Large searches are split into batches, and signed-in users can download every export again from the dashboard.

  • Export from the Apollo tab you are on, or run large exports in batches
  • Files named by date, Apollo filter name and batch
  • Stop an export at any time and keep what was collected
  • Delete an export, or your whole account, from the dashboard

Why “Labuyo”?

Siling labuyo is a small chili from the Philippines. It is tiny, it grows pointing up, and it is much hotter than its size suggests.

That is the idea behind the studio: small apps that each do one job, and do it properly. Our app names follow the kitchen too.

One job per app

Each app solves one problem. No bundles of features you will not use.

Your data, your call

No analytics or ad tracking. Delete an export or your whole account at any time.

Plain pricing

A free plan, one paid plan, and a 7-day refund if it does not work for you.
